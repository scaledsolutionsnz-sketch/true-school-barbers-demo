About the shop

A proper barbershop for Invercargill

True School Barbers keeps it simple: good cuts, honest chat and a chair you actually want to sit in. We're set up on Victoria Avenue in the middle of town, doing everything from tidy skin fades to classic short back & sides.

Whether you're in for a quick clean-up or the full cut, beard and hot towel, you'll get the time it takes to do it properly — no production line, no rush.
