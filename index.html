Cuts, fades & shaves

The shop

A proper barbershop, not a quick clip

True School Barbers is a local Invercargill shop built on the old-school idea that a haircut should be worth sitting down for. Mason and the crew take the time to get the shape right, tidy every line and send you out feeling sorted.

Whether it's a weekly skin fade, a beard shape-up or the full hot-towel shave, you get honest barbering, good chat and a clean chair every time.

  • Locally owned and run in Avenal, Invercargill
  • Walk-ins welcome, or book your chair online
