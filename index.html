About the shop

Invercargill's home for a proper cut

True School Barbers set up on Victoria Avenue in Avenal to give Southland blokes a barber chair worth coming back to. Pull up, tell us what you're after, and we'll sort the shape, the fade and the finish.

From the young fellas' first cut to a full wet shave before a big day, you get honest barbering, a good yarn, and a fresh look every time. Book your chair online and see exactly when we're free.

  • Every age welcome — kids, gents and everyone between.
  • Fades to straight razors — the full traditional barber range.
