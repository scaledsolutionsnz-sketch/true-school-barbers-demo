Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

This is placeholder wording for the demo website. True School Barbers should review and adjust this policy to reflect how the shop actually handles customer information before going live.

1. Who we are

True School Barbers is a barbershop based at 52 Victoria Avenue, Avenal, Invercargill. This policy explains how we handle any personal information you share with us, in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

2. Information we collect

We only collect the information we need to provide our service, which may include:

  • Your name and contact details when you book an appointment or get in touch.
  • Appointment history and preferences to help us look after you in the chair.
  • Basic, anonymous website analytics (such as pages visited) if enabled.

3. How we use it

We use your information to manage bookings, contact you about your appointment, and improve the service we provide. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't send marketing you didn't ask for.

4. Bookings & third parties

Online bookings are handled through our booking provider, and any details you enter there are covered by that provider's own privacy terms. If you email or message us, that message is stored with the relevant provider (for example Gmail or Facebook).

5. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and only keep it for as long as we need it.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Just get in touch and we'll sort it.

7. Contact

For any privacy questions, call 021 153 0128 or email us via the contact section of our website.
