Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

True School Barbers respects your privacy. This placeholder policy explains how we handle any information you share with us and should be reviewed before the site goes live.

What we collect

If you call, email or book with us, we may hold basic details like your name, phone number and any preferences for your cut — only so we can look after your booking and get in touch if needed.

How we use it

We use your details to manage appointments, answer enquiries and provide our services. We don't sell your information, and we don't send marketing you didn't ask for.

Online bookings

If you book online, your details are handled by our booking provider under their own privacy terms. We only access what we need to manage your appointment.

This website

This site links out to services like Google Maps and our social pages, which have their own privacy policies. We don't run intrusive tracking on this demo site.

Your choices

You can ask us what details we hold, or ask us to update or remove them, at any time — just get in touch.
