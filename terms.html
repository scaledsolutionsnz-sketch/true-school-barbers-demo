Bookings & walk-ins

We welcome both walk-ins and online bookings. If you've booked a chair, please arrive on time — if you're running late we'll do our best to fit you in, but we may need to rebook you so we're fair to everyone waiting.

If you can't make a booking, let us know as early as you can so we can offer the chair to someone else. Repeated no-shows may affect future bookings.
